ABOUT CREATORPAY

Learn from mentors. Grow your knowledge. Build your future.

CreatorPay is an online educational platform that helps learners discover educators, mentors and subject experts for educational guidance, mentoring sessions and knowledge-sharing services.

Our goal is to make it easier for learners to find suitable mentors, understand available educational services, review pricing and request guidance through a simple online platform.

What We Do

CreatorPay provides an online space where learners can:

  • Explore educator, mentor and subject expert profiles.
  • View mentor information, categories and educational expertise.
  • Review available mentoring or educational services.
  • Check session details and applicable pricing.
  • Request educational guidance or knowledge-sharing sessions.
  • Make payments through the supported payment gateway.
  • Contact our support team for platform-related questions.

Our Educational Platform

CreatorPay is designed to connect learners with people who offer educational knowledge, practical guidance and mentoring support.

Depending on the listed service, a mentor may provide subject guidance, career guidance, skill-based learning, study support, practical advice or other educational knowledge-sharing sessions.

The exact service, session format, duration, availability and price are displayed on the relevant mentor profile or service page.

Our Commitment

We aim to provide clear and useful information about mentor profiles, educational services, session details and pricing.

We encourage learners to review our Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy and Refund & Cancellation Policy before making a payment.

Payments

Payments on CreatorPay may be processed through a supported third-party payment gateway such as Razorpay. The final payable amount is displayed before payment confirmation.

CreatorPay does not ask users to share confidential payment information such as OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs or passwords.
