ABOUT FRIENDCONNECT

Connect with people who inspire you.

FriendConnect is a platform designed to help users discover creators, explore their profiles, and connect with them through available services.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make meaningful creator connections simple, accessible, and easy to explore.

FriendConnect brings creator profiles and their available services together in one convenient place. Users can explore creator information, review service prices, and choose the service that best matches their needs.

We aim to create a clear and user-friendly experience for both users and creators.

🤝

Meaningful Connections

We help users discover creators and explore opportunities for conversations, guidance, mentorship, and other available services.

🔍

Simple Discovery

Our platform is designed to make creator profiles, categories, service details, and prices easy to understand.

🔒

User Awareness

We encourage users to review creator information, service details, pricing, and applicable policies before making a payment.

How FriendConnect Works

Explore creator profiles, review the services they offer, select an available service, and follow the payment instructions shown on the platform.

Payments may be processed through Razorpay or another authorized payment service provider.
