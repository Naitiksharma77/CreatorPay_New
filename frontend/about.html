ABOUT CREATORPAY

Helping people discover new connections.

CreatorPay is an online platform that helps users discover creator profiles and connect with people based on their interests and availability.

Our platform provides profile information, pricing details and a simple process for users who wish to request a paid interaction or friendship-related service from a listed creator.

What we do

CreatorPay provides a convenient online space where users can:

  • Explore available creator profiles.
  • View profile information and service pricing.
  • Choose a creator based on their preferences.
  • Make payments through our supported payment gateway.

Our commitment

We aim to provide clear information about available profiles, pricing and payment-related details. We also encourage users to review the applicable terms, privacy policy and refund policy before making a payment.
