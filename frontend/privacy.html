CREATORPAY PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CreatorPay collects, uses, stores and protects information when you visit or use our educational platform, mentor profiles and knowledge-sharing services.

By using CreatorPay, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use CreatorPay, we may collect or receive the following information:

  • Name or profile information provided by you.
  • Email address and contact information.
  • Information submitted through support or contact forms.
  • Details about the mentor, educator or educational service you select.
  • Payment reference details and transaction status.
  • Technical information such as browser, device and usage data.
  • Information required to provide educational support or respond to your request.

2. Payment Information

Payments on CreatorPay may be processed through Razorpay or another supported payment service provider.

CreatorPay does not ask you to share your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN, CVV, banking password or other confidential banking credentials with us.

The payment provider may process payment information according to its own privacy policy and terms. CreatorPay may receive limited information such as payment status, transaction ID, order ID and other details needed to confirm or support a payment for an educational service.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To operate and maintain the CreatorPay educational platform.
  • To display mentor, educator and subject expert profiles.
  • To display educational services and knowledge-sharing information.
  • To process and verify payments.
  • To provide educational and platform-related support.
  • To respond to questions, requests or complaints.
  • To prevent fraud, misuse and security problems.
  • To improve website performance and user experience.
  • To maintain business and transaction records.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share limited information with trusted service providers who help us operate the website, process payments, provide hosting, maintain security or provide customer support.

Where necessary for a selected educational service, limited information may be shared with the relevant mentor, educator or service provider to help process or deliver the requested service.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, government request or when necessary to protect the rights, safety and security of CreatorPay, our users or others.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Cookies and Technical Information

CreatorPay or its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain website functionality, understand website usage and improve performance.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including educational service support, payment support, business records, dispute resolution, security and legal compliance.

8. Third-Party Services

CreatorPay may use or link to third-party services, including payment gateways, hosting providers, database providers and other technical services.

These third-party services may have their own terms and privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their policies before using those services.

9. Children and Minors

CreatorPay is an educational platform. Users who are not legally permitted to use paid online services must not make payments or use paid services without the permission and supervision of a parent or legal guardian, wherever required by applicable law.

If you believe that a minor has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so that we can review the issue.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about the information we may hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information or raise a privacy-related concern.

Requests may be subject to reasonable verification and applicable legal requirements.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.
