Refund Policy

Effective Date: 16 September 2026

This Refund Policy explains the conditions under which a refund may be requested for payments made through FriendConnect.

By making a payment on FriendConnect, you acknowledge and agree to this Refund Policy.

1. Payment Processing

Payments made through FriendConnect may be processed by Razorpay or another authorized payment service provider.

Once a payment is completed, the transaction may be subject to verification and processing by the payment service provider.

2. When a Refund May Be Considered

A refund request may be considered in situations such as:

  • The payment was deducted but the transaction was not successfully confirmed.
  • The same payment was accidentally completed more than once for the same service.
  • A paid service was not delivered due to a technical issue caused by FriendConnect.
  • A payment was made incorrectly and the issue is verified by our support team.
  • A refund is required under applicable law or the applicable payment service provider's rules.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

A refund may not be available in the following situations:

  • The user changes their mind after receiving or using the service.
  • The user provides incorrect information that prevents the service from being delivered.
  • The service was delivered as described by the creator.
  • The request is based only on dissatisfaction with a creator's personal opinion, style, or content.
  • The payment issue is caused by the user's bank, payment application, network, or payment provider, unless otherwise required by law.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact FriendConnect customer support using the contact details provided below.

Your request should include:

  • Name used during the transaction.
  • Email address or contact number.
  • Transaction ID or payment reference number.
  • Date and amount of the payment.
  • A clear explanation of the refund request.

Do not share your UPI PIN, card PIN, banking password, CVV, OTP, or other sensitive payment credentials with FriendConnect or anyone claiming to provide support.

5. Refund Review Process

Every refund request may be reviewed individually. We may verify the transaction details, payment status, service status, and other relevant information before making a decision.

Additional information may be requested when necessary to verify the request.

6. Refund Processing Time

If a refund is approved, it will generally be initiated through the original payment method or the applicable payment service provider.

The time taken for the refunded amount to appear in your bank account or payment account may depend on your bank, card issuer, UPI application, or payment service provider.

7. Failed or Pending Payments

If your bank account is debited but the payment status on FriendConnect is failed, pending, or not confirmed, please contact customer support with your transaction details.

We will check the payment status and guide you according to the result received from the payment service provider.

8. Chargebacks and Disputes

If you believe a transaction is unauthorized or incorrect, please contact us as soon as possible.

We may cooperate with the payment service provider, bank, or relevant authority to investigate payment disputes and resolve them according to applicable rules.

9. Changes to This Refund Policy

FriendConnect may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
