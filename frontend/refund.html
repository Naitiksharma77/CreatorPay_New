CREATORPAY POLICY

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At CreatorPay, we aim to provide a clear and reliable experience for users who book educational guidance, mentoring sessions, subject guidance or other learning services offered through our platform.

Please read this policy carefully before making a payment. By completing a payment, you agree to the applicable terms described below.

1. Nature of the Educational Service

CreatorPay may provide access to educational guidance, mentoring sessions, doubt-solving discussions, subject guidance, skill-development sessions, career guidance or other clearly described learning services.

The exact service, session details, mentor information and price should be displayed before the payment is completed.

Educational guidance is provided for learning and informational purposes. CreatorPay does not guarantee examination results, academic marks, admission, employment, certification or any particular educational outcome.

2. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered in the following situations:

  • Payment was deducted but the booking or service was not created.
  • The same payment was accidentally completed more than once.
  • CreatorPay is unable to provide the purchased educational service or mentoring session.
  • The scheduled session is cancelled by the mentor or service provider before it begins.
  • A technical issue prevents the user from accessing or receiving the purchased service.
  • The mentor or educator does not provide the service within the clearly agreed time without a reasonable alternative arrangement.
  • A refund is required under applicable law or payment provider rules.

3. Situations Where a Refund May Not Be Available

A refund may not be available in the following situations:

  • The purchased educational service has already been provided.
  • The mentoring session or educational interaction has already started.
  • The user changes their mind after the service has been delivered.
  • The user provides incorrect contact, booking or scheduling information.
  • The user does not attend the scheduled session or does not respond at the agreed time.
  • The user does not complete the required steps needed to attend or access the session.
  • The user violates applicable platform or service rules.
  • The issue is caused by the user's device or internet connection, unless otherwise required by law.
  • The user requests a refund only because the educational outcome did not meet their personal expectations.

4. Cancellation Requests

Cancellation requests should be submitted as soon as possible before the scheduled educational service or mentoring session begins.

Cancellation approval may depend on the nature of the service, whether the service has already started, the timing of the request, the mentor's availability and applicable legal requirements.

If a session is cancelled by the mentor or by CreatorPay, the user may be offered a rescheduled session or a refund, depending on the circumstances.

5. Rescheduling of Educational Sessions

If a mentor or educator is unable to attend a scheduled session, CreatorPay may coordinate a suitable alternative time where possible.

Users should contact support as soon as possible if they need to request a change in the scheduled session time. Approval of rescheduling will depend on mentor availability and the nature of the purchased service.

6. Failed or Pending Payments

If money has been deducted from your bank account but the payment status shows failed or pending, please contact us with the payment details.

We may check the payment status with Razorpay or the relevant payment provider. If the payment was not successfully received, the amount may be reversed or handled according to the payment provider's process.

7. Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, it will generally be processed through the original payment method used by the customer.

The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's bank account, card or payment wallet may depend on the payment gateway, bank, card issuer or UPI provider.

CreatorPay will provide available refund or transaction information when required for support or payment tracking.

8. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or report a payment issue, please provide the following details:

  • Customer name
  • Registered email address
  • Payment date
  • Payment amount
  • Payment ID or transaction reference
  • Name of the mentor or educator
  • Booked educational service or session details
  • Reason for the refund request

9. Fraudulent or Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe that a payment was made without your authorization, contact your bank or payment provider immediately.

You should also inform CreatorPay through our support email. We may request transaction information to investigate the issue.

10. Disputes Related to Educational Services

If you are dissatisfied with an educational service, mentoring session or guidance interaction, please contact CreatorPay support with the relevant booking and payment details.

We will review the matter based on the service description, booking information, communication records and applicable policy.

Any decision will be made subject to applicable law and the specific facts of the case.

CreatorPay may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
