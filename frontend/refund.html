Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At CreatorPay, we aim to provide a smooth and reliable experience for users who purchase paid interactions, content, or other services offered by creators listed on our platform.

Please read this policy carefully before making a payment. By completing a payment, you agree to the terms mentioned below.

1. Nature of the Service

CreatorPay may provide access to paid creator interactions, digital content, mentorship, community access, or other creator-related services.

The exact service and price will be displayed before the payment is completed.

2. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered in the following situations:

  • The payment was deducted but the order or service was not created.
  • The same payment was accidentally completed more than once.
  • CreatorPay is unable to provide the purchased service.
  • A creator cancels the service before it begins and a refund is approved by CreatorPay.
  • A technical issue prevents the user from receiving the purchased service.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

A refund may not be available in the following situations:

  • The service has already been provided or the interaction has started.
  • The user changes their mind after the service has been delivered.
  • The user provides incorrect information or an incorrect contact detail.
  • The user does not respond or is unavailable at the agreed time.
  • The user violates the platform rules or the applicable service terms.
  • The issue is caused by the user's device, internet connection, or payment-bank delay.

4. Cancellation Requests

Cancellation requests should be submitted as soon as possible before the purchased service begins.

Cancellation approval depends on the nature of the service, whether the creator has started providing the service, and the circumstances of the request.

5. Failed or Pending Payments

If money has been deducted from your bank account but the payment status shows failed or pending, please contact us with the payment details.

We will check the payment status with the payment gateway. If the payment was not successfully received by CreatorPay, the amount may be automatically reversed by the payment provider or handled according to the provider's settlement process.

6. Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, it will generally be processed through the original payment method used by the customer.

The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's bank account or payment wallet may depend on the payment gateway, bank, card issuer, or UPI provider.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or report a payment issue, please contact us with the following details:

  • Customer name
  • Registered email address
  • Payment date
  • Payment amount
  • Payment ID or transaction reference
  • Reason for the refund request

8. Fraudulent or Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe that a payment was made without your authorization, please contact your bank or payment provider immediately and also inform us using our support email.

We may request transaction information to investigate the issue.
